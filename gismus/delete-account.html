GisMus Account & Data Deletion

GisMus provides in-app account deletion for registered users. Deletion is intended to remove the user's account data from the GisMus service.

How to delete your account

  1. Open GisMus and sign in to your registered account.
  2. Open the account/settings area.
  3. Choose the account deletion option.
  4. Complete the confirmation steps shown by the application.

What deletion does

GisMus's account-deletion flow deletes the account's service data through the application's account-deletion process, including account-associated favorites and other user data covered by that deletion operation.

Local device data

Data stored locally on your device may require the application to clear its local session/storage as part of deletion or may remain until the application is removed or its local data is cleared. The application should not be treated as a guarantee that every device-level artifact is immediately removed by a server-side account deletion.

Backups and limited retention

Copies that exist temporarily in backups, logs, security records, or other infrastructure may be retained for a limited period where necessary for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, disaster recovery, or technical operations. Such information is not retained for ordinary product use after account deletion.

If you cannot access your account

If you cannot sign in or cannot complete the in-app deletion flow, use the current official GisMus support/contact method and request account deletion. Do not send passwords or other authentication secrets in a support request.
