GisMus Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 12, 2026

1. About GisMus

GisMus is a Gis application designed to help people discover nearby Masjeds and related community features in the United States and Canada.

2. Information we handle

Depending on the features you use, GisMus may handle account information needed to create and authenticate an account, saved favorites, application settings, and location information needed for location-based features such as finding nearby Masjeds and providing location-relevant functionality.

3. Account information

If you create an account, we use your account credentials to authenticate you and maintain your account. Passwords are intended to be stored and processed using appropriate security protections rather than as readable plaintext.

4. Location

Location is used when you choose features that require it, such as finding nearby Masjeds or using location-dependent functionality. Location access can be controlled through your device settings. GisMus is currently designed for use in the United States and Canada.

5. Favorites and settings

When authenticated features are used, information such as saved favorites and relevant account settings may be associated with your account so the features can work across sessions.

6. Notifications

If you enable notifications, the application may process the information necessary to deliver those notifications and maintain your notification preferences.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect information handled by the service. No method of transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Retention and deletion

You can request deletion of your account and associated account data through the account-deletion functionality provided by GisMus. See the Account & Data Deletion page for instructions and additional information.

9. Children

GisMus is not intended to require children to provide unnecessary personal information. If you believe personal information has been provided inappropriately, contact the service operator so the matter can be reviewed.

10. Third parties

GisMus may rely on infrastructure and platform services needed to operate the application, authenticate users, deliver notifications, or provide other requested functionality. Those providers process information according to their applicable terms and privacy practices.

11. Changes

This policy may be updated when the application, its data practices, or applicable requirements change. The effective date above identifies the current version.

12. Contact

For privacy or account-deletion questions, use the contact method provided with the GisMus application or its current official support channel.
